The feed, arriving.

We don't have a live lab feed yet, so this makes one. It replays the Tyks data and adds new patients, so results arrive day by day the way they would in a hospital: late, in stages, sometimes corrected. That lets us test the app on data that keeps changing.

Each dot is one result, landing on its ward at the day the sample was taken. The further it flies back, the later it arrived.
